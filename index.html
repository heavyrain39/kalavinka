About Kalavinka and how to listen

Kalavinka is a procedural music generator for work and study. Composition, synthesis and mastering all happen live in your browser, and a single seed weaves harmony, melody, bass and rhythm into music that keeps evolving.

Moods

Warm desk is lo-fi, Quiet space is ambient and After hours is electronic, with four-on-the-floor or drum & bass rhythms.

How to listen

Choose a mood and press Play. The sliders shape the music, and the instrument panel sets each part's sound and turns it on or off. New flow composes another piece.

Save and share

The heart saves the eight bars now playing, up to 12, and the link button shares the current music settings. Settings and saved music stay in this browser.

Press Space to play or stop. Closing the tab or putting your device to sleep may stop playback.
