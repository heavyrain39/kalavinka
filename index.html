About Kalavinka and how to listen

Kalavinka is a free procedural music generator for work and study. Its Web Audio engine synthesizes instrumental background music in your browser, without sign-in or a music generation API.

Three music moods

Choose Warm desk for lo-fi, Quiet space for ambient, or After hours for electronic music. After hours offers four-on-the-floor and drum & bass rhythms.

How to listen

Choose a mood and press Play. Adjust tempo, energy, warmth, variation, melody repetition, reverb and volume. Choose sounds and mute harmony, bass, rhythm, melody or arpeggio separately. New flow creates another composition.

Save phrases and share settings

The heart saves the current eight-bar phrase to this browser, with up to 12 favorites. The link button shares music settings, not the saved phrase or an audio recording. Settings and favorites use local browser storage; there is no account sync or audio file export.

Use continuous playback or a 25/50-minute focus timer. The player supports Korean and English, light and dark themes, and a focus view. Closing the tab or putting the device to sleep may stop playback.
